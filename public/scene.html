Fog of war (GM)
|
F toggles fog mode from anywhere on the page (GM only), or use the checkbox. Fog mode routes clicks to fog instead of tokens — tokens are untouched while it is on. Cover paints fog; Reveal erases it (drag inside existing fog to open a window). Drag for a rectangle or circle; for a polygon click each corner then press Enter (Esc cancels). With select: click a region to pick it (the most recent one under the pointer wins) · drag it to move · drag empty space (or Alt/Option+drag anywhere) to marquee-select · right-click for actions · T flips cover/reveal · arrows nudge · Ctrl/Cmd+A/C/X/V/D · Del deletes. Rendering is fog = everything covered, minus everything revealed, so a reveal is reversible — unlike a real eraser, you can flip it back to cover.
