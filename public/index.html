What this is

A real-time virtual tabletop for small groups: a game master and up to seven players share campaigns — scenes and tokens on a gridded map, fog of war, characters and inventories, an initiative strip, dice, and chat with whispers. Everything runs in the browser; the server is the single authority for what happens at the table. The rules engine is deliberately small: the system is D&D-inspired but simplified, and the table — not the software — decides what a roll means.

  • One shared board

    Token moves, fog reveals and scene changes reach every player live.

  • Dice you can trust

    Every roll happens on the server; your browser only animates the result.

  • The GM runs the table

    A hidden token never reaches a player's browser — what you can't see was never sent.

The thesis

This application is the artefact of an undergraduate thesis at the Department of Informatics, Ionian University: building a server-authoritative real-time application — authentication and session management, authorisation boundaries, and validation at every input.
