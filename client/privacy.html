Privacy

Last updated 7 October 2026.

Who is responsible

This app is an undergraduate thesis project (Department of Informatics, Ionian University), run by Alexandros Mpakodimos, who is the data controller. Contact: support@trucksart.com.

What we collect and why

  • Account: e-mail address, username, password (stored only as an Argon2id hash) and an optional profile picture. These are needed to create and use an account.
  • Game content: games, scenes, characters, items, spells, chat messages, dice rolls and images you upload or add by link. Needed to run your games.
  • Sign-in session: a record of which account a signed-in browser belongs to.
  • Security: your IP address in the web host's server logs. Rate limits count requests under a keyed hash of your IP address or account.

Legal basis: providing the service you sign up for (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)); server logs and rate limits rest on our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure (Art. 6(1)(f)). There is no profiling, advertising or automated decision-making.

Who sees or handles it

  • Other players in a game see what you post in it.
  • Service providers store or carry data for us: IP.GR (hosting and e-mail, Greece), Neon (database, Frankfurt), Render (short-lived data and rate-limit counters, Frankfurt) and Cloudflare (uploaded images, stored in its Eastern Europe region and delivered through its global network). Neon, Render and Cloudflare are US companies; Render's and Cloudflare's data-processing terms cover transfers outside the EU under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. Neon's terms: neon.com/dpa.
  • Password check: when you choose a password, our server sends the first 5 characters of its SHA-1 hash to the Pwned Passwords service to check it against known breaches. The password itself is never sent.
  • Images added by link are loaded by each player's browser from that website, which can see their IP address.

How long we keep it

  • Sign-in: 7 days, or until you log out.
  • E-mail links: verification 24 hours; password reset and e-mail change 1 hour.
  • Deleted games: 30 days in Recently deleted, then removed with their images.
  • Your account and content: until you delete them.
  • Rate-limit counters: at most one hour. Server logs: under IP.GR's policy.

On your device

One cookie, connect.sid, keeps you signed in for 7 days. It is strictly necessary for the service, so there is no consent banner. Your browser's local storage keeps your theme, dice colours and last "speaking as" choice; these are never sent to us. There are no analytics or third-party cookies.

Deleting your account

In My Account, use the bin icon. Transfer or delete any game you own first. Your account, sessions, memberships, profile picture and your games in Recently deleted are removed. In other people's games, your chat lines (under the name you used), your characters (unassigned) and the tokens and images you added stay, no longer linked to you, so those games stay intact.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to its use, and to receive a copy of it (portability). Most of this is in My Account; for the rest, e-mail support@trucksart.com. We answer within one month. You can also complain to the Hellenic Data Protection Authority (dpa.gr).

Accessibility

We aim for WCAG 2.2 level AA, as a voluntary target. Known limits: tokens and fog on the map need a mouse, and the pages are built for desktop and tablet screens, not phones. If something is hard to use, e-mail support@trucksart.com.
